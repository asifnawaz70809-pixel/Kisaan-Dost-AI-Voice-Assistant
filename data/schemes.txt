Scheme 1: Zarai Taraqiati Bank Limited (ZTBL) Loan
- Kisaan 5 se 10 lakh rupay tak qarza le sakte hain.
- Isme mark-up (interest) bohot kam hota hai.
- 2 acre se zyada zameen wale kisaan eligible hain.

Scheme 2: Punjab Agriculture Subsidy for Tractors
- 25 HP se 85 HP tak ke tractors par 10 lakh rupay tak ki subsidy milti hai.
- Kisaan ko apna CNIC aur zameen ke kaghazat (Fard Malkiat) dene honge.

Scheme 3: Solar Tube Well Scheme
- Kisaan apne khet ke liye solar tube well lagwa sakte hain.
- Bijli ke bill ki bachat hoti hai. 50% tak government kharcha uthati hai.
